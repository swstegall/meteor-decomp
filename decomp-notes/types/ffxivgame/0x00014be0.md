## Identifying signature

The 14-byte slot 3 has a unique fingerprint:

```
8b 44 24 04   MOV EAX, [ESP+4]
50            PUSH EAX
e8 ?? ?? ?? ? CALL FUN_009d56fd   (REL32)
59            POP ECX             ; cheaper than ADD ESP,4 by 2 bytes
c2 04 00      RET 0x4
```

The `MOV EAX,[ESP+4] / PUSH EAX` (5 B) + `POP ECX` (1 B) pairing — vs.
the shorter `PUSH [ESP+4]` (4 B) + `ADD ESP,4` (3 B) form — is a
recurring MSVC 2005 codegen choice for thiscall→cdecl trampolines that
don't need to retain EAX across the call: the `POP ECX` recovers the
extra byte spent on the explicit `MOV;PUSH`, netting 1 byte vs. the
`PUSH [mem]` path.

The 18-byte slot 1 and 21-byte slot 2 share the prologue / epilogue
shape with literal `PUSH 0x10` / `PUSH [ESP+8]` for the hard-coded
0x10 alignment passed to `_aligned_malloc`. Together the four slots
form a near-canonical "default allocator" surface.
