## Family-specific quirk vs the `ReceivableHeapSpace` ctor (0x00012870)

`ReceivableHeapSpace::ctor` (0x00012870, 126 B) writes **two** embedded
Link sentinels at +0x20 / +0x2c plus 6 user-supplied DWORDs at
+0x04..+0x18 and a memcpy fn-ptr at +0x1c — the CRITICAL_SECTION lives
at +0x38 there. `SystemHeapSpace::ctor` is the **single-Link** variant:
just one embedded Link at +0x20, just one user DWORD at +0x04, and the

## Identifying signature

The MI-style ctor fingerprint — write the embedded sub-object vftables
first, then the most-derived primary vftable — recurs across the
`SQEX::CDev::Engine::Memory::Alternative::*` family. For
SystemHeapSpace specifically, the 76-byte body opens with this very
recognisable burst:

```

56              PUSH ESI

```

followed by the single Link sentinel-init pattern at +0x20 and the
trailing pair of `MOV [ESI+0x34], <DebugSpace>` / `MOV [ESI+0x3C],
<DebugBlock>` debug-vftable writes. The terminal `8b c6 5e c2 04 00`
`MOV EAX,ESI / POP ESI / RET 4` epilogue confirms the
constructor-returns-this convention.

## How to find sibling ctors

Other `Alternative::*HeapSpace` classes (Receivable, Removable,
Separate, etc.) will have analogous ctors with the same shape:

1. PUSH ESI / MOV ESI, ECX prologue.
2. PUSH `&[ESI+CS_offset]` + `CALL [InitializeCriticalSection IAT]`.
3. Sequence of `c7 06 ...` / `c7 46 ...` vftable writes at the
   class-specific offsets — primary at +0, embedded Link(s) at +0x20
   (and possibly +0x2c for the 2-Link variant), debug sub-objects at
   their per-class offsets.
4. `MOV EAX, ESI / POP ESI / RET N` epilogue (N = 4 ×
   stack-arg-count).

The vftable VAs each ctor stamps disambiguate which class's ctor you've
found.
