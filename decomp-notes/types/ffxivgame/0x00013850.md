## Wider engine_memory family

The two trailing debug-vtable slots at +0x54 / +0x5c suggest
`DetachableHeapSpace` is a *composite* — the parent owns inline
debug shadows of itself + a sample block, presumably to satisfy
the `IDebugSpace` / `DebugDetachableHeapBlock` interfaces without
forcing every concrete subclass to allocate them. The `_memcpy`
pointer at +0x20 is the customisation point: callers (or the
class's vtable methods) invoke `this->copy_fn(...)` rather than
calling the CRT directly, allowing test fixtures or instrumented
builds to swap in a tracer.
